        Devices & Sensors

        Host policy over device classes. Simulated drivers are injected until real OS drivers land; killing a session stops it immediately.

        Relay & Interfaces

        Interfaces remain available for this device in Off mode; Bridge and Transport Node additionally forward peer traffic.

        Bluetooth is unavailable on desktop. Optical, acoustic, ntfy, I2P, and WebSocket relay diagnostics are exposed through the SDK when a corresponding host driver is installed.

        Settings

        Opt-in public Reticulum transport. The operator can observe your IP address and timing.
        Contracts enable freenet:contract for granted mini-apps. The HDLC interface carries Reticulum frames over a Freenet packet-log contract and needs a shared rendezvous with the peer. Off by default; mobile/web do not expose this. Updates published to Freenet cannot be recalled.

        Identity backup

        Anyone with your recovery words is you. Store them offline; TwistedPear cannot reset or revoke them.

        Safety

        Blocking drops future LXMF deliveries. Muting keeps messages but suppresses notifications. Reports stay on this device unless you export them.
