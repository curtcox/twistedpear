# Motivation

When you use a personal device to do something, it often involves far-away
computers that have no real logical need to be involved.

TwistedPear exists to change that, through two goals that reinforce each other:

1. **Users choose who is involved.** People decide if and when extraneous
   computers take part in what they are doing. In a world where TwistedPear is
   widely adopted, users always know who is involved in their activities — and
   whenever they don't know, they can easily find out.
2. **An app platform with no gatekeeper.** TwistedPear is a platform for
   running apps that do any sort of activity a user might want to do, on any of
   their personal devices. Unlike other platforms, users can always examine the
   source code of a program before running it, and no central authority decides
   which programs may exist or who may run them.

Neither goal works without the other. Choice about who is involved is hollow if
a platform gatekeeper decides what you can run; an open platform is dangerous
if users cannot see and control what programs do.

## Trust without a central authority

No central authority determines whether a program is safe. Instead, the
platform is built so that running a dangerous program *by accident* is
essentially impossible:

- Programs must publish their source code.
- Programs must carry a verified signature from their author.
- Programs must declare beforehand what resources they need access to (such as
  the camera) and why.

Trust is not one decision, and it does not transfer automatically. Trusting a
person does not mean trusting the app they wrote. The user ultimately decides
whether an app is safe to run, whether they want to run it, and what it is
permitted to do while running. They don't have to make those decisions alone
or repeatedly — the platform and community can help — but published source and
a verified author signature are always required.

## Non-goals

- There is no intention to make money for the platform providers or the
  application authors.
- There is no intention to market the platform. Adoption comes only from
  whatever word-of-mouth spread happens as a byproduct of satisfied users.

## What success looks like

Success is ordinary people, on whatever personal devices they use, choosing
TwistedPear because it lets them know and control who is involved in what they
do — even if that adoption grows only by word of mouth.
